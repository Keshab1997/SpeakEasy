SpeakEasy v1.0.27 — Fair Play & Trophy Protection 🛡️

🛡️ TROPHIES CAN NEVER TRAP YOU
• Rookie Shield: trophies never drop below 0.
• Rank protection: once you reach a division (Challenger, Master,
  Grandmaster) you can never fall back below its starting line.
• Loss-streak shield: every 4th loss in a row costs ZERO trophies —
  a bad streak can't ruin your climb.
• Comeback bonus: winning while under 100 trophies gives +30 instead
  of +25, so getting back on your feet is faster than ever.
• Result screen now celebrates your shield saves and comeback wins.

🎯 FAIR PLAY
• Your opponent's chosen answer is now hidden until YOU submit your
  own answer — no more peeking at the other player's choice.

🔧 FIXES
• Fixed a match sometimes starting on its own right after you pressed
  Cancel during opponent search — cancel now truly stops matchmaking,
  removes you from the queue, and never launches a bot match.
