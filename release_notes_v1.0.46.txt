• A live duel request expires after 90 seconds and an offline (async) one after 48 hours — expired requests can never pop up, show as "Pending ⏳", or block your buttons again.
• Expired requests (and their duel rooms) are erased from the server — no ghosts left behind.

• Declining now cancels the duel room instantly and erases every trace of the request moments later.
• The sender is properly told "the player declined" — then the request vanishes for good.
• Accepting one challenge still auto-declines the others, and those are now fully cleaned up too.

🧹 TIDIER ARENA
• The Duel button unlocks the moment a request expires — no more waiting on dead "Pending ⏳" states.
