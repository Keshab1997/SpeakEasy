SpeakEasy v1.0.43 — Battle Arena: Reliable Rematches & Fair Duels ⚔️

🤺 RE-CHALLENGE AFTER DECLINE NOW WORKS
• Challenging a player again after they declined once no longer breaks the duel — accepting now instantly joins the fresh room.
• Fixed a false "player left the room" message that appeared even though the opponent had actually accepted your challenge.
• Decline detection is now double-checked against the server, so a slow network can never kill a healthy duel room.

❓ SAME QUESTIONS FOR BOTH WARRIORS
• Fixed: duels could show DIFFERENT questions to each player — both warriors now always battle on the exact same 5-question set, every single match.
• New version-mismatch warning: if you and your opponent are on different app versions, the waiting room warns you BEFORE the duel starts.

⚡ STABILITY & IMPROVEMENTS
• Smarter automatic retries when joining a freshly created duel room on slow connections.
• Safer challenge cleanup under weak network — no more stuck "Joining…" dead ends.
